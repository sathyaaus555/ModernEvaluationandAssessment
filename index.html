Topic 01 · The foundation

Introduction to Modern Assessment

5 MIN

Assessment is more than assigning grades. It is the process of gathering evidence about what students know, what they can do, and where they need support.

Traditional vs Modern Assessment

Traditional approach

Assessment of learning

Often focused on measuring achievement at the end of a unit or course.

  • Emphasis on final examinations and marks
  • Feedback may arrive after the learning activity
  • Frequently uses the same test for the whole class
  • Primarily answers: “What did the student achieve?”
Modern approach

Assessment for learning

Uses evidence and feedback throughout the learning journey to guide improvement.

  • Combines quizzes, tasks, projects and exams
  • Gives timely, actionable feedback
  • Identifies misconceptions and learning gaps
  • Also asks: “What should happen next?”
Important: Modern assessment does not eliminate examinations. It combines well-designed final evaluations with ongoing checks and useful feedback.

Three essential assessment approaches

✓
During learning

Formative

Short, low-stakes checks that help teachers and students adjust learning while it is happening.

Example: A three-question exit quiz after a Python loops lesson, followed by immediate feedback.
★
After learning

Summative

Evaluates achievement against learning outcomes at the end of a topic, unit or course.

Example: An end-of-semester exam or graded project assessing Python programming skills.
↻
Across learning

Continuous

Collects evidence of progress regularly using several assessment opportunities over time.

Example: Weekly quizzes, lab tasks, feedback and project milestones tracked across a semester.

The modern assessment cycle

01Define outcomesWhat should students be able to demonstrate?
02Gather evidenceUse questions, assignments and authentic tasks.
03Give feedbackExplain strengths and next improvements.
04Adjust teachingAddress gaps and assess progress again.

One classroom example

Exam-only approachPython loops

Students learn loops for several weeks and receive a score after a final test. Misunderstandings may be discovered late.

Continuous-feedback approachPython loops

Students answer a short quiz after the lesson, receive targeted feedback, practise weak areas and demonstrate improvement in a later task.

Knowledge check · Audience participation

Which assessment is most useful for identifying learning gaps during a lesson?

Choose an option, then reveal the explanation. This is a local practice question, not a live multi-participant poll.

Correct answer: B · Formative assessment.
Formative checks provide evidence while learning is underway, so the teacher can identify gaps and adapt instruction before the final evaluation.
